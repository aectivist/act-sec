The PJPT certification is a beginner-level penetration testing exam experience. This exam will assess a student’s ability to perform an internal network penetration test at an associate level. Students will have two (2) full days to complete the assessment and an additional two (2) days to write a professional report.

In order to receive the certification, a student must:

1) Leverage their Active Directory exploitation skillsets to perform lateral and vertical network movements and ultimately compromise the exam Domain Controller
2) Provide a detailed, professionally written report.
